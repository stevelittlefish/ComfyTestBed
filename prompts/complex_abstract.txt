An intricate non-representational abstract composition existing in no real space: thousands of interlocking translucent geometric filaments and Möbius ribbons woven into a self-referencing lattice, threaded through with fractal spirals and impossible Penrose junctions. Iridescent glass tendrils refract light into shifting spectra; concentric fields of fine moiré interference ripple across the surface; nested clockwork of gears that mesh at impossible angles. A restrained but luminous palette of deep indigo, teal, molten gold and pearlescent white against void-black. No recognisable objects, no landscape, no figures — pure structural complexity, dense detail, mathematical elegance, hypnotic depth, flawless rendering.
